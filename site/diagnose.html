Check the empty feed

This temporary check helps identify why posts are hidden. It does not change your feed settings.

  1. Install the diagnostic.

    Open this link in Safari, then page menu → Userscripts → Install. Keep your usual Instagram script enabled.

    Install layout check
  2. Open Instagram Home and reload.

    Wait for the empty feed, tap Copy report in the small panel, and paste it into our chat.

Reports contain layout counts and the installed script version. No cookies, account names, messages or post text. Nothing is sent automatically.

After sending the report, disable Instagram — layout check in Userscripts.
