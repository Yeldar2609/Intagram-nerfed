# Classic icon attribution

`instagram-classic.png` is the exact 512 × 512 PNG supplied by the user for this project. It replaces the previously requested 2016 gradient logo.

The build removes transparent outer padding and fits the artwork proportionally into transparent square PNGs at 32, 48, 128, 180, and 512 pixels. The source artwork is preserved unchanged.

The Instagram name and artwork belong to their respective owner. This is an independent personal project and does not claim affiliation with Instagram or Meta. No additional license is asserted for the user-supplied artwork.
